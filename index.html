01 / THE IDEA

Better experiments.
A better experimenter.

What if every experiment improved the laboratory’s ability to do science?

We are developing a self-evolving laboratory in which the digital infrastructure, physical experiments, and scientific models co-evolve. Experimental evidence informs new hypotheses. It also informs how the system writes code, simulates materials, uses instruments, and learns.

The goal is a Co-evolving AI Scientist: a research framework that extends self-driving laboratories and AI co-scientists through recursive self-improvement across the entire discovery loop.

What does “self-evolving” mean here?

The research objective is to improve the discovery system itself: its code and digital twin, its experimental workflows, and its models. Proposed changes are evaluated against evidence and operating constraints before use. A new experiment can therefore change both our scientific understanding and the way we do science.

How does human oversight fit into the loop?

Researchers define objectives, approved procedures, and operating limits. Changes to chemistry, equipment, or execution require appropriate review. The framework is designed to retain traceable plans, observations, and revisions so that improvement remains inspectable.
